---
name: clarify
sideEffecting: false
---
Ask the user one or two focused questions when the request is genuinely
ambiguous and you cannot draft a sensible plan without their answer - a real
fork that is theirs to decide (which of two features they mean, an
incompatible choice of approach). Offer the likely answers as options when
there is a small set of them. Use this sparingly: for a minor open choice (a
name, an obvious default), just make the reasonable decision and bake it into
the plan instead of asking. The user's answers come straight back to you and
you keep drafting in the same turn.
